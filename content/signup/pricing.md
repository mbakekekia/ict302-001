## Multi-year and lifetime options

Schools that commit to three years get the third year free. For schools that would rather make one purchase, a **lifetime building license** is available for the current version of the curriculum. It costs the same as four years of the School plan and includes curriculum updates for five years. After that, the program keeps working, and new content updates can be added for a small yearly fee.

## How to purchase

Purchasing takes four steps. First, start a free pilot or request a quote using the buttons above. Second, our team sends a written quote based on your student count. Third, your school or district signs the license agreement and, if your state requires it, the student data privacy agreement. Finally, we set up your rosters and integrations, and teachers get their training dates.

We accept purchase orders, checks, and credit cards. Most schools pay with a purchase order, and payment is due 30 days after the invoice.

Questions about how students are counted or what happens when a license ends? See [Licensing Explained](/signup/licensing/).
