Licensing is meant to be simple, but there are a few things every school should know before signing.

## Who is counted

Licenses are counted by enrolled student, not by device or by login. A student who uses the program at school and at home still counts once. Teachers, aides, administrators, and parents never count toward the total and can be added for free.

Your student count is set at the start of the license period from your roster. If enrollment grows during the year by more than 10 percent, we send a small true-up invoice at the end of the year. If enrollment drops, the price does not go down until renewal.

## What a license includes

Every license includes the full curriculum for the grades you choose, the AI tutor, the teacher dashboard, updates released during the license period, and standard support. School and District licenses add training and integration setup, as shown on the [pricing page](/signup/pricing/).

## Using the program at home

Students can log in from home with the same account at no extra cost. Families can see their child's progress report but cannot change any settings. The AI tutor follows the same rules at home as at school, and teachers can turn home access off for any class.

## Renewal and cancellation

Licenses renew yearly unless the school cancels at least 30 days before the end date. If a school does not renew, teachers keep read-only access to reports for 90 days so they can download anything they need. After that, student data is deleted, as described on the [technical details page](/tech/technical-details/).

## Lifetime licenses

A lifetime building license gives one school permanent use of the version of the program it bought. It includes five years of curriculum updates. After five years, the program keeps running and the school can choose whether to pay for new updates. Lifetime licenses cannot be moved to another school.

## Security packet and agreements

Before buying, IT and legal teams can request our standard license agreement, the student data privacy agreement for your state, and a security packet with our data flow diagram and audit summary. Use the request button on the [pricing page](/signup/pricing/) and say which documents you need.
