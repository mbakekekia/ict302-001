## Key takeaways

Every answer a student gives starts the loop again. A quick check shows what they know, the program works out which specific skill or misconception explains the answer, and the next task changes to match. That might mean an easier problem, a harder one, or a worked example before trying again.

The last step is what makes this program different. Before a skill counts as mastered, the student has to explain their thinking in their own words. Research on tutoring systems found that the most effective ones work step by step and give feedback on each step of reasoning, not just on the final answer. Asking for an explanation also makes it much harder to copy an answer without understanding it.
