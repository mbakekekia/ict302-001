## Key takeaways

Students are already using AI for school, and that use keeps rising. The real question for schools is what kind of AI students use.

The Bastani study gives a clear answer. AI that hands over answers makes practice feel easier but leaves students worse off on the test. AI that gives hints helps during practice without that drop.

Teachers are worried about dependence and are losing trust in student work, and many are turning to detection tools that can wrongly accuse honest students. A platform that keeps students doing the thinking, and shows teachers how the work was built, addresses both problems.
