Families trust schools with their children's information, and schools need to know that any AI tool they adopt will protect it. AI Innovation Lab K-12 is designed around four privacy commitments.

## School-managed accounts

Students do not sign up on their own. Every student account is created and managed by the school, usually through the school's existing Google, Microsoft, Clever, or ClassLink sign-in. Students never need a personal email address, and there are no public profiles, chat with strangers, or advertising.

## Only the information that is needed

The platform collects only what it needs to teach: the student's name, grade, class enrollment, and the work they do in activities. It does not collect home addresses, phone numbers, location, or anything unrelated to learning. AI features see the current activity and the student's work on it, not the student's full record.

## School control over access and data deletion

The school decides who can see student data. Teachers see their own classes, principals see their building, and families can see their own child's progress reports. Districts can export all of their data at any time and can delete a student's data or an entire school's data on request. When a license ends, student data is deleted within 90 days unless the district asks for it to be kept longer.

## Student work is never used to train AI models

Student work stays with the school. It is not used to train or improve the AI models the platform uses, by us or by any AI provider we work with, and this is written into every school's contract.

## Designed to meet student privacy laws

The platform is designed to comply with FERPA and COPPA, and with state student privacy laws, including signing each state's student data privacy agreement where one exists. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. For more on how the platform approaches responsible AI use, see [Teaching Responsible AI Use](/research/responsible-ai-use/).
