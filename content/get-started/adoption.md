Most schools move from first look to full classroom use in about one semester. Here is how it usually goes.

{{< steps >}}
1. **Introduce the platform to administrators.** A principal, curriculum director, or technology coordinator gets a 30-minute live demo and the privacy and security packet. This is when the school decides whether the platform fits its goals and its AI policy.
2. **Review available learning activities.** A small team of teachers explores the activity library for their grade levels and subjects, using a free 60-day pilot account for up to three classrooms.
3. **Select a curriculum unit.** The team picks one upcoming unit to start with and builds a unit pack of games, simulations, and evidence tasks that match it. Starting with a single unit keeps the first rollout manageable.
4. **Train teachers.** Teachers complete a two-hour training, live or on demand, on setting up classes, using the [teacher controls](/educators/teacher-controls/), and reading student progress. Schools on the School plan or above get two live sessions included.
5. **Begin classroom implementation.** Teachers launch the first unit with students. After four weeks, the school reviews progress reports and teacher feedback, then decides which units and grade levels to add next.
{{< /steps >}}
