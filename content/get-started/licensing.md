AI Innovation Lab K-12 is a product concept. The licensing structure below shows how the platform would be licensed if it were available. It is not an actual offer to purchase.
{{< /callout >}}

## Annual licenses

The main license is an annual subscription. A Classroom license is counted per teacher. School and District licenses are counted per enrolled student, so a student who uses the platform at school and at home still counts once. Teachers, aides, administrators, and families are never counted and can be added for free.

The student count is set at the start of the year from the school's roster. If enrollment grows by more than 10% during the year, the school receives a small adjustment invoice at the end of the year. Licenses renew yearly unless the school cancels at least 30 days before the end date.

## Lifetime building license

For schools that would rather make one purchase than budget for a subscription each year, there is a lifetime building license. It gives one school building permanent use of the platform version it buys, priced at four times the annual School plan for the number of students enrolled at purchase. It includes five years of new activities and updates. After five years, the platform keeps working, and the school can choose whether to pay a small yearly fee for new content.

A lifetime license belongs to the building, not the district, and cannot be moved to another school.

## What every license includes

Every license includes all three AI tools, the full activity library for the licensed grades, teacher controls, progress reports, accessibility features, and standard support. School and District licenses add integration setup and live teacher training, as shown on the [pricing page](/get-started/pricing/).

## When a license ends

If a school does not renew, teachers keep read-only access to their reports for 90 days so they can download anything they need. After that, student data is deleted, as described on the [privacy page](/educators/privacy/).
