## What happens next

For a demo, we will send a few times for a 30-minute video call. For a free pilot, we will set up accounts for up to three classrooms and send a getting-started guide. For a quote or documents, we will send them by email, including the license agreement, the student data privacy agreement for your state, and our security overview.
