## Lifetime building license

One payment of four times the annual School plan, based on enrollment at purchase. For a school of 400 students, that is $24,000 once, compared with $6,000 every year. It includes five years of updates. See [Licensing Information](/get-started/licensing/) for the details.

## How we set these prices

These are sample prices for a product concept, based on what comparable K-12 AI tools charge. Khan Academy's AI tutor Khanmigo was priced at $35 per student per year for districts after a 2023 price cut ([THE Journal](https://thejournal.com/articles/2023/11/16/khan-academy-cuts-district-price-of-khanmigo-ai-teaching-assistant.aspx)). MagicSchool's individual Plus plan costs about $100 per teacher per year when billed annually ([Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/magicschool-ai/pricing)). Brisk Teaching uses custom per-student pricing for schools and districts ([Brisk](https://briskteaching.com/plans)). AI Innovation Lab K-12 sits near the teacher plans for single classrooms, and below Khanmigo's district price for whole schools.
