description = 'Three AI learning tools that help students practice, test their own ideas, and back up what they say with evidence.'

AI Innovation Lab K-12 is built around three tools. Each one uses AI to guide students instead of doing the work for them, and each one gives teachers a clear view of how students are thinking. Students can move between all three in a single unit: practice the skill, test an idea with it, then prove what they found.
