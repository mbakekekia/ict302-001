## What the feature does

The Evidence and Explanation Coach helps students decide whether a claim is true and explain why. It works with claims from anywhere: a sentence in a textbook, a headline, something a chatbot said, or a claim in the student's own essay. Students gather evidence, judge how strong it is, and write a short explanation of what they concluded. It is built for grades 3 to 12, with simpler claims and sources for younger students.

## How students evaluate claims

Students start by restating the claim in their own words. Then they collect evidence for and against it. For each piece of evidence, the coach asks three questions: Who made this? How do they know? Does it actually support the claim, or just sit next to it? An evidence strength meter fills in as students add sources, so they can see at a glance whether their case is strong or thin.

## How AI directs students toward approved sources

The coach does not search the open internet. Teachers and school librarians build a list of approved sources for each unit, such as library databases, government sites, and class texts, and the coach points students to those first. When a student pastes in something from outside the list, including an AI chatbot answer, the coach marks it for checking and asks the student to find where that information originally came from.

## How students identify missing or unreliable evidence

The coach is designed to find gaps, not fill them. If a student's sources show that two things happen together but not that one causes the other, the coach flags "missing evidence" and asks what kind of study would answer the question. If a source has no author, no date, or no original data, the coach asks the student whether they would trust it and why. Students finish by writing an explanation in their own words, and the coach gives feedback on their reasoning without rewriting their sentences.

This is where students learn the most important skill for living with AI: not trusting a confident answer just because it sounds confident.
