## Key takeaways

The three tools are designed to be used together. A student practices a skill in AI Learning Games, uses it to test an idea of their own in the Project Testing Lab, and then backs up what they found with the Evidence and Explanation Coach. Each step asks a little more of the student's own thinking.

In all three tools, the AI's job is the same: ask questions, give hints, and point out gaps. It never hands over the answer. The teacher decides how hard the work is, which resources and simulations are available, and when students can use AI at all.
