## Key takeaways

Step-by-step computer tutoring comes close to the learning gains of a human tutor, which is the strongest reason to bring it into classrooms where one-on-one help is not possible.

Students are already using general AI chatbots for school, and that use doubled in a single year. Most teens see AI as fair for research but not for writing their essays for them, which matches how the program is designed.

Teachers who use AI regularly are saving real time each week. That time can go back into small group instruction and one-on-one conversations with students.
